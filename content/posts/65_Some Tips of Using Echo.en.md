[A few days ago I introduced the basics of Echo](/zh-tw/posts/2026/09/echo-reading-to-writing/), and today I want to share a few tips that make it even easier to use.

### Tip 1: Add a sentence with a keyboard shortcut
![](/images/Post/65/1.webp)
By default, the + key adds a new sentence. Once I've finished adding one sentence, I just press `Shift` + `+` to quickly add the next one.

### Tip 2: Customize the shortcuts
![](/images/Post/65/2.webp)
If you don't like the shortcuts I set as defaults, you can go to "Preferences" → "Keyboard Shortcuts" at the top and set the keys you prefer!

### Tip 3: Fill in the source quickly
![](/images/Post/65/3.webp)
In the source field, just press the `↓` key to bring up the last five entries you typed. When you're adding quote after quote from the same book, you don't have to retype the same information over and over.

### Tip 4: Create folders
![](/images/Post/65/4.webp)
If you want to sort the quotes you've collected, click the + next to Folders, then select the quote cards and drag them over!

On top of that, readers have probably noticed that Echo has a dark interface! Just head to "Appearance" to switch it; you can also change the language under "Preferences."
