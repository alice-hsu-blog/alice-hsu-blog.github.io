I recently watched two anime, *Sakamoto Days* and *Haven’t You Heard? I’m Sakamoto*. They feature two very different kinds of heroes, and both are great!

### *Sakamoto Days*
A crime boss hanging up his gloves is hardly a new premise, but I think *Sakamoto Days* gives its characters a special charm. The protagonist, Taro Sakamoto, has a body a bit like Luffy’s: he can swing between fat and thin at will, which I find deeply enviable. And the way this tough guy is afraid of his wife and dotes on his daughter makes him feel like a well-rounded, lovable hero. I’d like to track down the live-action movie next.

### *Haven’t You Heard? I’m Sakamoto*
*Haven’t You Heard? I’m Sakamoto* is surreal and weirdly addictive. Its protagonist is a high schooler who is handsome, smart, kind, and thoroughly *chūnibyō* all at once. At the critical moment, a single “yare yare” (やれやれ) is enough to turn danger into safety, though the way he solves problems is so wonderfully absurd that I can never see it coming. And even though Sakamoto is indeed a hero, I just can't have any romantic feelings for him, because his *chūnibyō* power is simply too strong. He often leaves me speechless, and yet I can’t resist the next episode, which is probably the most addictive thing about the show.

Besides the main character, the character who left the deepest impression on me is Kubota’s mom. She somehow manages to be scarier than the haunted-house episode, making her the MVP of the entire series. The background music in each segment is also impressive: it gets stuck in your head while dressing up pure comedy in a heartwarming disguise.

I highly recommend both!
