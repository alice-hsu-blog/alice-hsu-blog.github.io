## The Geese

Four or five flocks of geese were scattered across the lake. Some dipped their faces underwater to forage, some wiggled their rears to shake off the water, and some tidied their feathers with their beaks.

They murmured in low, short calls, and every so often one would stretch its neck and let out a long “Honk——”

Suddenly, a stir.

Ga-honk—— ga-honk——

All together they fluttered, kicking up spray across the lake, and flew off high into the distance. Black and white dots streaked across the sky, becoming daytime shooting stars.

## The People
### 1
A middle-aged man in sunglasses sat on a park bench wearing AirPods, cradling a white poodle with fluffy ears. The poodle leaned against his arm, languid and melancholy, a contrast to its owner’s easy contentment.

### 2
An old woman, alone, pushed a wheelchair slowly to a large tree and ate from a lunchbox beneath it. When she finished, she stared into space for a while, then scrolled on her phone for a while, surrounded by the shrieks and clamor of children. Had the years been gentle with her? I couldn’t say. I only knew that, for this moment, her time was still.


### 3
On a slightly chilly evening, I went for a walk with a jacket over my shoulders. I stepped into a patch of sunlight, and its warmth slipped through the fine fuzz of the fabric and settled against my back. I couldn’t help looking forward to the next patch of light. A slight chill, it turns out, is the best temperature for bringing out the warmth of the sun.

## The Scene
Beside the dock stood a small sign that read:

“In loving memory of xxx. Let’s go fishing together again someday, Dad.”

Rewind a few decades. A child holding a fishing rod has just hauled a bluegill out of the lake. Bouncing with joy, the child turns to a tall figure and says:

“Dad, let’s go fishing together again sometime! Next time I’m gonna catch a big one!”

The father pats the child’s head, turns, and picks up his rod, a cooler in his other hand. One large back and one small gradually fade into the distance.
