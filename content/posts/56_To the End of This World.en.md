![](/images/Post56home.jpg)

To the end of this world,

only to find

I have arrived at someone else’s home,

while my own home

has become a traveler’s final stop.

---

To move forward is, in truth, to retreat, returning

to someone else’s daily life, bending, bowing back

into someone else’s old age, receding

to the very point where someone else once set out.

---

My home has become the end of the world,

and I must set out again.

---

Note: Written after reading Hu Ching-fang's *The Traveler*.
