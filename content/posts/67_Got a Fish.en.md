This evening Sam and I went fishing at the lake with one of his coworkers, and I finally caught the first fish of my life!
![](/images/Post67home.jpg)

I'd always come back empty-handed with artificial lures, but today I borrowed some live bait from his coworker, and I finally got a bite!

Once it was on shore, it flopped around like crazy. Sadly, before I could get a photo of me holding up the line, it wriggled right off the hook.

Hoping to catch a big one next time!
