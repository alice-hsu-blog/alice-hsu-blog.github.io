I recently discovered a fantastic fitness resource called [Darebee](https://darebee.com/). It has no fees, no ads, and no sponsored content. It runs entirely on donations, a nonprofit site kept going by a group of people out of pure love.

So what makes it so great? So far, the site has collected 2,800 workouts, a full 2,400 of which require no equipment at all. They cover strength training, cardio, HIIT, boxing, stretching, and more, and if you own dumbbells or kettlebells, there are workouts for those too. Every workout is presented as an illustration in one consistent style, simple and easy to follow.

![](/images/Post/75/2.png)

I love the idea behind the whole site: you don’t need a pricey gym membership, fitness equipment, or an expensive app subscription. As long as you know the movements, you can start exercising anytime, anywhere. You don’t even need an account to access the entire database. Just go to the site, check off the type, difficulty, and body part you want to work on, and the matching illustrations and instructions appear. Some come with a YouTube video, and if one doesn’t, no problem: a quick YouTube search will show you how it’s done.

Since there are so many workouts, if you don’t know where to begin, I recommend starting with the [“My Current Fitness Level”](https://darebee.com/test.html) test at the bottom of the homepage. It’s an equipment-free test that takes about 8 to 10 minutes, and it will show you what difficulty to choose.

![](/images/Post/75/3.png)

If you’d like a schedule to follow, choose Training Plans or Programs from the top of the site. Training Plans last about a week, while Programs run for about a month. I’m currently doing [Recomp](https://darebee.com/plans/recomp.html) from Training Plans. It combines strength training and HIIT and works the whole body, and I really like it.

Darebee was founded in 2012, long before generative AI appeared. Its workout plans are all designed by real people and drawn in Photoshop, and even now that AI is so convenient, they still refuse to use it to design their plans.[^1] I truly admire the commitment of founder Neila Rey and the team, and the love and faith that have kept the site running all these years.

After using Darebee for a while, I realized it had given me back ownership of my exercise. Instead of relying on a gym trainer or hoping some viral workout will change me, I choose what fun thing I want to do today: Strength or cardio? Upper body or core? With dumbbells or without? Does that make me dependent on this website? Well, you can long-press to save its images and view them on your phone offline. Better yet, after doing some workouts for a while, you memorize them and don’t need to open the site at all.

This is how exercise should be: plain, simple, and effective. I hope you’ll head over to [Darebee](https://darebee.com/) and find a workout you love.
