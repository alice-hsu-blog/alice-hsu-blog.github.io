New year, new beginnings. In order to revamp my image (?), I recently changed my YouTube and SoundCloud handles and set up two new Gmail accounts.

My YouTube and SoundCloud handles, which were originally `@alice_h_hsu`, are now `@alicehsumusic`. It’s clear and straightforward, representing me making music.

As for my Gmail accounts, I previously had an address called alicemusicwonderland77 for music-related things and emails from this blog. However, I eventually felt it was too long and unprofessional. So, I went ahead and created alicehsumusic@gmail.com specifically for music-related matters. Additionally, I applied for another Gmail account, alicehsuwrites@gmail.com, to handle blog correspondence and writing-related tasks. Any emails sent to mail@alicehsu.blog (displayed on my homepage) will also be automatically forwarded to this address.

Nowadays, finding a Gmail address that looks professional and cool without having to add any numbers is truly difficult! I’m so happy to have found these two unused accounts.

In short, here is how to contact me:
1. Music-related:
Please email alicehsumusic@gmail.com
1. Writing/Blog-related:
Please email alicehsuwrites@gmail.com or mail@alicehsu.blog

That said, if I write a music-related post and you want to respond to the content, feel free to pick either one lol. Either way, the person receiving the email will always be me.

To all my silent readers out there, come email me and say hi!
