The winter sun

dries out

my damp, heavy soul,

lifting it, shaking it loose.

Under the patting of daylight,
the musty scent slowly evaporates.

The soul begins to give off
a warm, sunlit fragrance.

I pick it up and slip it on.
Ah, letting out a satisfied sigh.

This kind of gentle touch,
paired with a cup of herbal tea,
and an easy conversation
with someone I love,

this must be
what blissfulness feels like
when it leans in for a kiss.

Yes, yes.
blissfulness is right there,
in a quiet corner
with sunlit steps.

------

This poem was originally written in Chinese and posted on Facebook on December 21, 2018. I never expected that exactly seven years later, on December 20, 2025, I would suddenly think of it again. So I decided to give it a proper home on my blog and translated it into English. 

At the time, it was the rainy season in Taipei, and it was also a rainy season in my life. That day, however, the weather was unexpectedly beautiful. I was studying in a library when I noticed this scene. Being moved, I decided to write this poem.

Reading it again years later still moves me. I am grateful for my younger self----during that season of rain, still striving to hold on to the sunlight, so I can arrive here.

I hope this poem can offer a bit of strength to anyone who is currently in their rain season.
