As the name suggests, Post-Vacation Syndrome (PVS) is the collection of behaviors and feelings people often experience after coming back from a trip.

Common symptoms include:
1. Scrolling through your vacation photos every single day.
2. Walking tens of thousands of steps while traveling, then feeling completely lazy at home.
3. Out traveling, I felt like a rock. At home, I melted into a puddle.
4. Constantly craving the food you had while traveling.
5. Losing motivation to work.

How to cope:
1. Think of three things you actually enjoy about being at home.
2. Take a good look at your wallet.
3. ~~Plan your next trip.~~
