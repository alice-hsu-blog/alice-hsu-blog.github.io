As a Christian, apart from reading the Bible, the most important part of my daily life is prayer. Prayer actually has many different facets, and one of the best known is bringing the things you need before the Lord Jesus. Besides praying for myself, I also pray for the needs of my family and friends, bringing the things that are too hard to solve on my own before the most capable Creator, and asking Him for help.

This morning, when I got back from church, it suddenly hit me: hey, I could pray for my readers too. How had I never thought of that before?

So I decided to set up a Google Form, so that you, as a reader, whether or not you're a Christian, can use it to ask me to pray for you.

If you're going through something difficult at work, in your life, in your relationships, and so on — big or small — you're welcome to ask me to pray. You can stay anonymous, and everything you share will be kept confidential; none of it will ever appear in a post on my blog.

Also, the form doesn't collect your email by default. So if you'd like a short email reply from me about your prayer request, you can enter your email at the end, and I'll write back to you personally by the end of September 15 (US Central Time). If you'd rather not, I'll simply pray for you quietly.

Honestly, I'm not sure whether anyone will need this, so I'll try it for a week first. The form closes on September 13 (US Central Time), and if people do fill it in, I'll open it again later.

Click here to fill it in: [Google Form link](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSd-sMky-Belie3z-oXmwgWqoIN_Q14MNWCEiVi9dCX57Z6xnQ/viewform?usp=dialog)

**Update, 2026/09/18**: **I've removed the deadline, and the prayer project has officially become a long-term project on this blog.** If there's anything you'd like prayer for, you can still click the link above and ask me to pray for you. If you'd like a reply, I'll write back within a week of receiving your message.
